1. Subscription vs. One-Time Choice Paralyis

Current layout displays two massive, structurally identical price boxes. This forces cognitive choice before value is fully established.

2. Lack of Sensory & Taste Trust

Mushrooms can be intimidating to drink. Current product images focus on the canister instead of describing taste, texture, and ritual.

3. Detached CTA & Missing Trust Anchors

On mobile, the "Add to Cart" button is easily pushed below the viewport. Key purchase triggers (Free Shipping, guarantee) are too low.

Proposed Wireframe Solution

04 / 05
1

Unified Subscription Toggle

Replaced the bulky double price blocks with a clean switcher toggle. Default is preset to "Subscribe & Save" showing clear $20 monthly savings.

2

Sensory Flavor Profile Bar

Added a visual taste/sensory meter (e.g., Cacao base, Earthy/Spiced notes, 1/7th Caffeine scale) directly under the image to align expectations immediately.

3

Sticky Bottom CTA & Mini-Trust Badges

The CTA locks to the bottom viewport on scroll. Key tags ("Free Shipping" & "30-Day Return") are placed directly under the action button to remove purchase friction.

Bulky Pricing Blocks

Canister Focus Only

Hidden CTA On Scroll

ACTIONABLE PLAYBOOK

Growth Execution Plan

Prioritized action checklist categorized by Conversion Impact and Engineering Effort.

05 / 05
Priority 1

⚡ Quick Wins

  • **Re-align Trust Anchors**: Move "Free Shipping" & "30-Day Guarantee" directly under the primary CTA button.
  • **Embed Taste/Flavor Meter**: Implement a simple static ingredient scale to quickly describe flavor profile notes.
  • **Dynamic Review Snippets**: Show 1-2 positive reviews containing terms like "taste," "easy morning routine" near CTA.
Priority 2

🚀 Strategic Roadmap

  • **Unified Pricing Toggle**: Replace bulky pricing blocks with a clean radio-style subscribe/one-time toggle.
  • **Sticky Bottom Action Bar**: Develop mobile-optimized sticky checkout footer that activates when scrolling past main fold.
  • **Interactive Ingredient Audit**: Create a modal breakdown detailing functional mushroom benefits (L-Theanine, Lion's Mane).
Priority 3

✨ Layout Polishes

  • **Improve Font Contrast**: Increase font weight on ingredient details list from 300 to 450 to meet mobile readability standards.
  • **Icon Standardisation**: Replace emojis with clean, uniform vector-based SVG icons.
Priority 4

🔬 Future Experiments

  • **Personalization Survey**: Add a 2-step onboarding funnel (e.g. "Do you want focus or energy?") to pre-select starter blends.
  • **Micro-Animations**: Add subtle CSS hover and transition effects to checkout tags and pricing options.
