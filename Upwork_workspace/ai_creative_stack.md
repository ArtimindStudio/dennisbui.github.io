# AI Creative Stack

A curated list of 'best-in-class' AI tools for the AI-Native Creative Strategist. This stack enables faster, cheaper, and scalable eCommerce ad production.

## 1. Video Generation
For generating cinematic B-roll, product shots, and dynamic motion.
- **Runway Gen-3 Alpha:** Best for hyper-realistic video generation and precise camera controls. Great for product macro shots.
- **Kling AI:** Excellent alternative for long-duration video generation and highly complex physical interactions (e.g., pouring liquids, holding products).
- **Luma Dream Machine:** Fast, high-quality output for general B-roll and atmospheric shots. Good for extending existing clips.

## 2. Image Generation
For static ads, precise storyboards, and background assets.
- **Midjourney v6:** The gold standard for photorealism, precise styling, and typography integration. Best for static Meta ads and UGC aesthetics.
- **Flux (by Black Forest Labs):** Best open-weight model for exceptional prompt adherence and generating legible text/logos on products. 

## 3. AI Avatars & Voice
For generating "founder figures" or scalable UGC-style actors without hiring talent.
- **HeyGen:** Industry leader for photorealistic AI avatars, precise lip-sync, and translating videos into multiple languages. Perfect for talking-head TikTok ads.
- **ElevenLabs:** Unmatched for ultra-realistic text-to-speech. Features conversational pacing, emotional inflection, and the ability to clone your own (or brand) voices.

## 4. Script & Copywriting
For breaking down ad strategies, writing hooks, and iterating on direct-response copy.
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet:** Best for writing long-form, highly conversational, and nuanced ad scripts. It excels at breaking down transcripts and strategic analysis without sounding cliché.
- **GPT-4o:** Excellent for rapid brainstorming, generating short punchy ad copy variations, and processing multimodal inputs (e.g., analyzing screenshots of competitor ads).
