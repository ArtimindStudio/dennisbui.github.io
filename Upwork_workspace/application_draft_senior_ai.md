### Question 3: How do you prevent AI videos/photos from looking like it's AI?
*Response*:
By using Google Omni's physical constraint mapping to ensure lighting directions, contact shadows, and reflections are physically accurate. We then apply classic post-production adjustments in Photoshop (match-grading grain, ambient occlusion, and micro-contrast) to eliminate vector textures and keep details looking sharp and real.

### Question 4: What is an uncommon piece of knowledge you've learnt about AI image/video generation?
*Response*:
Lighting consistency dictates realism far more than pixel resolution. If the direction, temperature, or softness of the light source on the product differs by even 5 degrees from the background environment, the human brain instantly registers the image as fake, no matter how sharp it is. Proper lighting matching is the key to invisible AI.
