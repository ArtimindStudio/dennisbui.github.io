# Hand-over: AI Visual Content Producer (JOB-PRODUCER-001)

## 1. Project Context
- **Job Title**: Freelance AI Visual Content Producer (Food/Education Sector)
- **Airtable ID**: JOB-PRODUCER-001
- **Client Goal**: A monthly "Production Sprint" delivering 15–20 high-quality assets (stills + short-form videos) for an Irish food business serving schools.
- **Audience**: School principals (Value/Reliability) and Parents (Trust/Warmth/Safety).
- **Core Requirement**: "Genuine Craft." The client hates "generic AI" or "stock-style" output. They want on-brand visual work that feels human-led and high-end.

## 2. Established Branding DNA: "Values-Led Warmth"
To win this bid, the demo assets must deviate from the "Big Brand / Neon" style and move toward:
- **Aesthetic**: Cinematic, warm, authentic, and trustworthy.
- **Food Photography**: Natural lighting, high-fidelity textures (freshness), and "Invisible AI" (zero plastic sheen).
- **Tone**: Aspirational but grounded. It must feel like a trusted local business, not a cold global corporation.
- **Consistency**: The entire 20-asset batch must look like it was shot in the same kitchen/environment during a single day.

## 3. Technical Workflow: Multi-Modal "Invisible AI"
- **Google Antigravity**: For "Brand DNA Mapping." Essential for locking the "Warmth" and "Lighting DNA" across both stills and video.
- **Google Nano Banana 2**: The primary 4K engine for photorealistic food stills. Focus on subsurface scattering on fresh ingredients.
- **Video Stack (Runway/Kling)**: For 3-4 short-form reels. Must maintain the same color grade and lighting as the stills.
- **Audio (ElevenLabs)**: For conversational, warm voiceovers that speak directly to parents.

## 4. Demo Asset Goals
- **Still 1: The Trust Shot**: A fresh school meal (e.g., roasted chicken/veggies) with natural window lighting and soft-focus background.
- **Still 2: The Action Shot**: A child/student (realistic, diverse, zero uncanny valley) enjoying a meal with genuine expression.
- **Video Reel**: A 10-second cinematic B-roll of fresh ingredients being prepared, ending with a warm title card.

## 5. Strategic Positioning
We are pitching as a **"Visual Producer with Heart."** We emphasize **Creative Judgment**—knowing when to use AI and when to use restraint to protect the brand's reputation with parents and educators.
